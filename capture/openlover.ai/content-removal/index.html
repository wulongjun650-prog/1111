Content Removal Policy

At OpenLover AI, we are committed to maintaining a respectful and safe environment for all users. We understand that some content may not follow our guidelines or may be deemed inappropriate. This policy explains the procedures and circumstances under which content may be removed from our platform.

Types of Removable Content

  • Content that violates our Community Guidelines.
  • Content that infringes intellectual property rights.
  • Content that is illegal, harmful, or abusive.
  • Content involving impersonation or unauthorized use of personal information.

Reporting Mechanism

  • Users can report inappropriate content through the reporting system available on each content page.
  • Reports can also be sent directly to our support team at market@openlover.ai.

Review Process

  • All reported content is reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours.
  • During the review, content may be temporarily removed or restricted until a final decision is made.

Consequences of Violation

Repeated violations of this Content Removal Policy may result in account suspension or termination.

Contact Information

For any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact us at market@openlover.ai.
